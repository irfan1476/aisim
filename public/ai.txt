The AI Investment Challenge
https://aisim.teachmeai.in/

Purpose
A 12-quarter, browser-based executive learning simulation for practising AI transformation and investment decisions. It is designed for reflection and replay, not for real-world prediction or professional advice.

Who it helps
Aspiring and current AI, transformation, product, operations, and business leaders; facilitators; and executive-learning participants.

Core learning loop
Choose a context -> select 0-3 initiatives -> allocate a finite campaign purse -> observe operating and scenario effects -> inspect analytics and causal explanations -> reflect -> replay with a different strategy.

What learners can control
- Standard mode or an optional operating scenario.
- A flexible, finite campaign budget and deployment pace.
- Portfolio size (zero to three initiatives), operating allocations, and reserve carry-forward.
- Decisions across data, people, infrastructure, MLOps, compliance, and innovation.

Scenarios
- Project Factory 2030: cross-functional AI transformation.
- BankNext Transformation: banking, fraud, credit, compliance, trust, and digital adoption.
- Care360 Health Network: healthcare access, safety, privacy, operations, and workforce pressures.
- FutureReady University: student engagement, faculty workload, employability, and change adoption.

Evidence and analysis
The simulation exposes initiative maturity, risk, readiness, adoption, spend, scenario progress, causal chains, synergies, crises, recommendations, decision patterns, and replay comparisons. Board Advisor responses remain evidence-based when no optional LLM provider is configured.

Boundaries
- Educational simulation only; not financial, medical, legal, regulatory, investment, or operational advice.
- Scenario metrics and modelled analytics are learning tools. They do not represent live organizational data or guarantees of outcomes.
- Some framework and forecast views are modelled proxies; they should be described as such.
- Browser saves are local unless a deployment explicitly adds persistent storage.
