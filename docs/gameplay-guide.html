The AI Investment Challenge · player guide

How the boardroom loop works

You are the Chief AI Officer of Project Factory 2030. Each quarter, decide where to place a finite transformation budget, then learn from the operating consequences. The goal is not to maximize technology spend — it is to create durable value.

focal decision / value creation campaign milestone risk event / crisis branchEach quarter is self-paced — no forced timer.
01 · Make the trade-off

Choose a portfolio

  • Select up to 3 initiatives from 6.
  • Balance cost, ROI, risk, data readiness, and human impact.
  • Allocate exactly 100% of the $10M envelope.
02 · Watch the system

Read consequences

  • ROI and revenue show financial value.
  • Adoption, satisfaction, and literacy show people impact.
  • Risk, compliance, and data readiness show resilience.
03 · Play the long game

Optimize for the verdict

  • Respond to crises without abandoning the strategy.
  • Use CFO, CTO, CHRO, or Risk perspectives.
  • Finish all 12 quarters to receive your CEO rating.
The winning pattern is rarely “pick the highest ROI card.” It is investing in the operating system around AI — people, data, MLOps, and trust — so the value compounds.
