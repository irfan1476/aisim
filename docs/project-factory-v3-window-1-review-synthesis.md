## Overall recommendation

Retain the authored three-priority research storyboard as the base. Adopt the
stable-shell clarity and limited energy acknowledgement from the reviews, but
do not adopt invented metrics, an immediate energy pilot, a recommended answer,
or operating benefits from research.

The learner-facing flow remains:

`Orient → Compare → Commit → Outcome → Reflect → Next Window`

## Corrected Window 1 orientation

### Three headline signals

- Unplanned downtime: **12.0%**; target ≤7.0%.
- Escaped defects: **1,240 PPM**; above the 1,000 PPM internal escalation
  threshold.
- Workforce readiness: **52/100**; target ≥70.

### Monitored context

> Also monitored: energy intensity is 15% above the prior baseline. Energy,
> demand, and supply-chain priorities remain on the portfolio horizon and will
> be reconsidered as evidence and capacity change.

This acknowledges the wider system without creating a fourth equal decision or
suggesting that energy is hidden.

## Corrected Compare-state portfolio visibility

Below the three decision packets:

~~~text
Later-window portfolio: Energy optimisation · Demand forecasting · Supply risk
[Why are these not on today's board agenda?]
~~~

The explanation is limited to one line per initiative:

- Energy: material pressure, but telemetry/production-context readiness is
  unresolved and the current board mandate prioritises continuity risks.
- Demand: schedule adherence is material, but its first signal takes two
  quarters and the initial agenda is focused on plant reliability, quality,
  and workforce continuity.
- Supply: depends on a named procurement response workflow and broader
  schedule/supplier evidence.

These initiatives are visible but not selectable in Window 1. A walkthrough
should test whether learners interpret this as legitimate board framing or
artificial restriction.

## Corrected Commit contract

For predictive-maintenance research:

- capital: ₹0.25 Cr;
- capacity: Data Engineering 1/4 and Governance Assurance 1/2;
- no pilot/scale delivery slot consumed;
- PF-E01 and PF-E02 cited;
- structured prediction:
  - evidence will support a constrained pilot;
  - more remediation will be required; or
  - this will prove to be the wrong priority;
- optional note only;
- explicit preview: operating reliability is not expected to improve during
  research.

Quarterly run cost must not be presented as automatically incurred at research
unless the content contract explicitly declares it.

## Corrected Outcome contract

The outcome may report only:

- lifecycle movement (`Deferred → Research completed/reviewed`);
- capital and capacity consumed;
- evidence gained, challenged, or still missing;
- pilot readiness or remediation requirement;
- unchanged operating metric(s), with an explanation of why no operating
  benefit was expected.

Any numeric research-result movement requires an authored, reviewed rule and
deterministic fixture before it appears. It must not be invented in a
wireframe.

## Visual-wireframe gate

Proceed to visual wireframes only after approving these recommendations:

1. retain the three opening research priorities;
2. acknowledge energy as monitored context, not a fourth equal choice;
3. show other priorities as a non-selectable later-window portfolio line;
4. preserve structured prediction and optional note;
5. preserve the research-stage no-operating-benefit boundary.

The visual wireframe should prototype all six states in one stable shell. It
should not change content or resolver behavior.
